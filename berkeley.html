United States

UC Berkeley

The earliest U.S. deadline, and firm. Berkeley uses the University of California application, not the Common App. Grades and activities are self-reported, there are no recommendations, and test scores are not considered.

Applying to
A specific college and major

What to apply to

Apply to one college and major. College of Engineering: Engineering Physics; Civil & Environmental; EECS (admitted directly to the major; EECS is extremely selective). Letters & Science: Applied Mathematics or Physics, declared after prerequisites. College of Computing, Data Science, and Society: Statistics or Data Science.

Supplemental essays

  • Self-reported grades from 9th–11th grade
