U of T application plan

Apply on OUAC by Nov 7, then finish the Engineering Supplemental Application (ESA) and documents by Dec 1. That early package guarantees an Engineering decision by April 30.

Programs to apply to

ESA rankProgramCompetitive range*Why
1Engineering ScienceLow to mid 90sTwo broad years, then a major: Engineering Physics; Math, Statistics & Finance; Machine Intelligence; Energy Systems; and others
2Electrical EngineeringLow to mid 90sPhysics-heavy: electronics, energy systems, photonics, signal processing and ML
3Industrial EngineeringHigh 80s to low 90sStatistics, operations research and data analytics; the realistic backup
4Civil or Mechanical (optional)High 80s to low 90sCivil for water and environment; Mechanical for physics

These four are ranked inside one OUAC choice (U of T Engineering). The second OUAC choice is Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical Sciences (TPG): applied math, physics, statistics or math, chosen after first year; mid to high 80s; no supplement.

*% average in prerequisite courses. TrackOne (undeclared engineering) is paused for 2027 entry. All engineering programs offer PEY Co-op, a paid 12–16 month placement after third year.

Co-op opportunities

U of T co-op is one long paid placement, not Waterloo-style rotating terms. Engineering students apply through the program; it does not affect admission.

ProgramCo-op routeLength and timingDetails
All Engineering, incl. Engineering Science, Electrical, IndustrialPEY Co-op (Professional Experience Year)12–16 months, full-time, after 3rd yearAbout 70% of 3rd-year engineers take it; average pay about $50,000 a year, top roles up to $90,000; 350+ employer partners; 12 months count toward the P.Eng. licence
All EngineeringSummer internship (ESIP)About 4 months, summer after 2nd or 3rd yearOptional; can be added to PEY for up to about 20 months of paid work
Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical SciencesArts & Science Co-op (by major)4, 8, 12 or 16 months, after 2nd and 3rd yearAvailable in selected majors; check the major he picks after first year

Sample PEY employers: NVIDIA (U.S.), Intel, Amazon, IBM, Google and Environment and Climate Change Canada. Indicate PEY interest on OUAC and confirm it in the ESA.

Compared with Waterloo's six 4-month work terms starting in first year, U of T gives one deeper placement later, with fewer separate chances at U.S. internships.
