    Canada

    U of T and Waterloo share one OUAC application; McGill is separate. All three charge domestic tuition to Canadian citizens.

    University of Toronto

    OUAC + Engineering Supplemental Application
    Apply by
    Nov 7 on OUAC; ESA and documents Dec 1 (early), Jan 15 final
    Programs
    Engineering ranked Engineering Science, Electrical, Industrial, Civil or Mechanical; plus Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical Sciences
    Co-op
    PEY: one 12–16 month paid placement after third year
    U of T plan

    University of Waterloo

    OUAC + Admission Information Form
    Apply by
    Nov 7 on OUAC (same form as U of T); AIF Feb 15, or Feb 1 with engineering
    Programs
    Mathematics (most likely admit), Computer Science, and Electrical or Computer Engineering
    Co-op
    4–6 rotating four-month terms, up to two years of paid work
    Waterloo plan

    McGill University

    Direct application, grades self-reported
    Apply by
    Jan 15; scholarship Jan 21; documents Mar 1
    Programs
    B.Sc. Physical, Earth, Math & Computer Science (applied math, physics, statistics, AI); Electrical Engineering second
    Tuition
    Quebec-resident rate, about $3,100 CAD a year, for applicants born in Quebec
