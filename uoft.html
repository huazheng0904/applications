University of Toronto

Three U of T choices on OUAC by Nov 7: Engineering Science, Computer Science and Mathematical & Physical Sciences. Finish the Engineering Supplemental Application (ESA) and documents by Dec 1 for a guaranteed Engineering decision by April 30; the Computer Science supplement is due Feb 1.

Programs to apply to

These four are ranked in the ESA under one OUAC choice, Engineering Science (TK), listed with Machine Intelligence as the major of interest and PEY co-op.

ESA rankProgramCompetitive range*Why
1Engineering ScienceLow to mid 90sTwo broad years, then a major: Engineering Physics; Math, Statistics & Finance; Machine Intelligence; Energy Systems; and others
2Electrical EngineeringLow to mid 90sPhysics-heavy: electronics, energy systems, photonics, signal processing and ML
3Industrial EngineeringHigh 80s to low 90sStatistics, operations research and data analytics; the realistic backup
4Civil or Mechanical (optional)High 80s to low 90sCivil for water and environment; Mechanical for physics

*% average in prerequisite courses. TrackOne (undeclared engineering) is paused for 2027 entry.

Other U of T choices on OUAC

  • Computer Science, St. George (TAD): low 90s; needs a separate CS supplementary application by Feb 1 (a to-do in Join U of T)
  • Mathematical & Physical Sciences, St. George (TPG): applied math, physics, statistics or math, chosen after first year; mid to high 80s; no supplement. The safer U of T option

Co-op

U of T co-op is one long paid placement, not Waterloo-style rotating terms. Engineering students apply through the program; it does not affect admission.

ProgramRouteLength and timingDetails
All EngineeringPEY Co-op (Professional Experience Year)12–16 months, full-time, after 3rd yearAbout 70% of 3rd-year engineers take it; average pay about $50,000 a year; 350+ employers; 12 months count toward the P.Eng. licence
All EngineeringSummer internship (ESIP)About 4 months, after 2nd or 3rd yearOptional; with PEY, up to about 20 months of paid work
Arts & Science Math & Physical SciencesArts & Science Co-op (by major)4–16 months, after 2nd and 3rd yearSelected majors only

Sample PEY employers: NVIDIA, Intel, Amazon, IBM, Google, Environment and Climate Change Canada.
