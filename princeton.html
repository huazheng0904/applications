Princeton University

Princeton is test-optional for Fall 2027 entry (required from the next cycle). It is the only school on the list that asks for a graded written paper.

Regular Decision deadline
Jan 1
Testing
Test-optional this cycle; send scores
Application
Common App
Applying to
B.S.E. (engineering) or A.B.

What to apply to

Choose the B.S.E. (engineering), with majors such as Operations Research & Financial Engineering (ORFE), Electrical & Computer Engineering, or Civil & Environmental Engineering; or the A.B. for Mathematics or Physics.

Supplemental essays

PromptLimit
B.S.E. applicants: why engineering, including any engineering experience250 words
Your Voice 1: how your lived experiences will shape respectful conversations, and what you will teach classmates400–500 words
Your Voice 2: how your story connects to Princeton's commitment to service250 words
More About You: a new skill to learn; what brings you joy; a song for your life right now50 words each
Graded written paper: an analytical paper from English, history or social studies in the last three years, with grade, teacher comments and prompt1+ pages

Princeton says not to trim an essay written for another school.

Also required or recommended

  • Alumni interview: optional, by invitation
