Waterloo application plan

Waterloo uses the same OUAC application as U of T, so its three choices go on that form by Nov 7. The real work is the Admission Information Form (AIF): due Feb 15 for Math and CS, or Feb 1 if an engineering program is listed.

Programs to apply to

OUAC allows three Waterloo choices; Waterloo does not look at how they are ranked.

ChoiceProgramCompetitivenessWhy
1Mathematics (co-op)Most likely admit; admits typically mid-90sOne entry to the Faculty of Math; major later: Applied Math, Mathematical Physics, Statistics, Data Science, Combinatorics & Optimization
2Computer Science (co-op)Reach; minimum 90% Grade 12 averageAI/ML route; same AIF as Math; contests weigh most here
3Electrical or Computer Engineering (co-op)Reach: EE and CE share about 360 spots with identical odds, about 66% at 95+ and 22% at 90–94Pick whichever to study, since neither is easier; adds Jan 15 deadline, Feb 1 AIF and video interview

Alternatives for choice 3: Physical Sciences (co-op), which also leads to Mathematical Physics with no engineering deadlines; or Environmental Engineering, an easier engineering admit (2023: about 95% admitted with 95+ averages, 77% with 90–94).

The AIF has five short questions (about 150 words each): a passion or interest, community involvement, a time someone was treated unfairly, goals at Waterloo, and an activities chart with hours for 2026. Links in the AIF are not reviewed, so the substance must be in the text.
