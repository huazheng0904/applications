Canada

University of Waterloo

Waterloo uses the same OUAC application as U of T: Mathematics and Computer Engineering go on that form by Nov 7. Because Computer Engineering is listed, the Admission Information Form (AIF) and video interview are due Feb 1.

Programs to apply to

Two Waterloo choices on OUAC; Waterloo does not look at how they are ranked.

ChoiceProgramCompetitivenessWhy
1Mathematics (co-op)Most likely admit; admits typically mid-90sOne entry to the Faculty of Math; major later: Applied Math, Mathematical Physics, Statistics, Data Science, Combinatorics & Optimization
2Computer Engineering (co-op only)Reach: shares about 360 spots with Electrical at the same odds, about 66% at 95+ and 22% at 90–94Adds the Jan 15 engineering deadline and the video interview; moves the AIF to Feb 1

One AIF covers both choices. Computer Science was considered and left off.

Requirements (U.S. high school)

The AIF has five short questions (about 150 words each): a passion or interest, community involvement, a time someone was treated unfairly, goals at Waterloo, and an activities chart with hours for 2026. Links in the AIF are not reviewed, so the substance must be in the text.

Contests affect scholarships, not admission. The Euclid contest is written in early April.

Transcripts and scores
