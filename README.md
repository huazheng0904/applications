# U of T Application Plan (Fall 2027)

Timeline, to-dos and program choices for a University of Toronto application from a U.S. high school. The site is `index.html`, served with GitHub Pages.

## Key dates

| Date | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Nov 7 | OUAC application submitted |
| Dec 1 | Engineering Supplemental Application (ESA) and all documents (early consideration) |
| Jan 15 | Final Engineering deadline |
| Feb 1 | Arts & Science documents due |
| Mar 31 | Residence application |
| Apr 30 | Engineering decision guaranteed by |

## Programs

**OUAC choice 1: U of T Engineering**, with ESA ranking:

1. Engineering Science (low to mid 90s)
2. Electrical Engineering (low to mid 90s)
3. Industrial Engineering (high 80s to low 90s)
4. Civil or Mechanical, optional (high 80s to low 90s)

**OUAC choice 2: Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical Sciences (TPG)**, mid to high 80s.

Ranges are % averages in prerequisite courses. TrackOne is paused for 2027 entry.
