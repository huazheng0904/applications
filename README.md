# Fall 2027 Applications

Timeline, to-dos and program choices for Fall 2027 university applications in Canada and the U.S. Served with GitHub Pages.

## Editing the site

Every page is generated. Edit the data in `schools.py` (or layout in `build.py`, styles in `style.css`), then run:

```
python3 build.py
```

`index.html` is the overview, `us.html` compares the U.S. schools, and each school has its own page (`uoft.html`, `penn.html`, ...).

## Programs

**OUAC choice 1: U of T Engineering**, with ESA ranking:

1. Engineering Science (low to mid 90s)
2. Electrical Engineering (low to mid 90s)
3. Industrial Engineering (high 80s to low 90s)
4. Civil or Mechanical, optional (high 80s to low 90s)

**OUAC choice 2: Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical Sciences (TPG)**, mid to high 80s.

Ranges are % averages in prerequisite courses. TrackOne is paused for 2027 entry.

## Co-op

| Program | Route | Length and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Engineering | PEY Co-op | 12–16 months full-time after 3rd year; about 70% take it; average pay about $50,000 a year |
| All Engineering | ESIP summer internship | About 4 months, summer after 2nd or 3rd year |
| Arts & Science Mathematical & Physical Sciences | Arts & Science Co-op, by major | 4–16 month terms after 2nd and 3rd year |

U of T co-op is one long placement; Waterloo uses six rotating 4-month terms.

## Publishing

Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root.

## McGill

See `mcgill.html`. Apply directly by Jan 15 (documents Mar 1; Major Entrance Scholarship Jan 21).

1. B.Sc., Physical, Earth, Math & Computer Science group: target Applied Math, Physics, Statistics, or a joint major with Computer Science (AI/ML via Mila)
2. B.Eng. Electrical Engineering (free second application)

Quebec-resident tuition (about $3,100 CAD/year) applies to applicants born in Quebec.

## Waterloo

See `waterloo.html`. Same OUAC application as U of T; AIF and documents due Feb 15. No engineering program, so no Jan 15 deadline or video interview.

1. Mathematics (co-op): Applied Math, Mathematical Physics, Statistics, Data Science
2. Computer Science (co-op), reach (replaced Computer Engineering, Nov 6)

## Euclid contest prep

See `euclid.html`. Euclid is Tuesday, April 6, 2027; a school must register the student. About 30 hours of prep, mostly Feb 16–Mar 31.

## U.S. schools

Submitted early: Columbia (ED, decision mid-December) and Northeastern (EA, decision by Feb 15). See `us.html` and each school's page. Regular Decision: UC Berkeley (Nov 30), then Harvard, Princeton, Dartmouth, Yale, Cornell, MIT, Duke, CMU, Rice, UChicago, Brown, Penn and Stanford (Jan 1–5).
