Tuition

Applicants born in Quebec qualify for Quebec-resident tuition as a permanent status. Proof is a Quebec birth certificate from the Directeur de l'état civil marked "Certifié conforme" and issued after January 1994. French is not an admission or graduation requirement for these programs.
