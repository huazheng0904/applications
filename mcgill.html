McGill application plan

Apply directly to McGill (not through OUAC) by Jan 15. Admission is based on grades and test scores; U.S.-school applicants self-report grades instead of sending transcripts.

Tuition

2026-27, 30 creditsPer year (CAD)
Quebec resident$3,117.60
Canadian, non-Quebec$12,985.50 or more (Engineering adds a supplement)

Applicants born in Quebec qualify for Quebec-resident tuition as a permanent status. Proof is a Quebec birth certificate from the Directeur de l'état civil marked "Certifié conforme" and issued after January 1994 (not the "article 137" version). French is not an admission or graduation requirement for these programs.
