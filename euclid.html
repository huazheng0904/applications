Registration

Students can't register themselves. A school must order the contest through CEMC's Contest Supervisor Portal and supervise it; students in 85+ countries write it.

Ask your own school's math department to register. If it won't, find a nearby school that already writes CEMC contests; you must write at the school that ordered it.

Resources

  • Euclid past contests, solutions and prep materials — use the last 8–10 years
  • 2026 Euclid — baseline paper
  • CEMC Grade 11–12 resources and Courseware — logs, trig, sequences, functions
  • Art of Problem Solving: Introduction to Counting & Probability, Number Theory, Geometry (optional)

For each paper: write it under contest conditions, mark it with the official solutions, rewrite every missed solution a day later, and keep a one-page formula and tricks sheet.

Test-day strategy

  • First 10 min: read all ten questions; mark what you can do
  • Q1–6 (~75 min): steady work; double-check answer-only parts
  • Q7–10 (~60 min): attempt every part; clear partial solutions earn marks
  • Last 15 min: check answers and solution write-ups
