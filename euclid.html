Euclid contest prep

The Euclid is on Tuesday, April 6, 2027: 10 questions, 2.5 hours, 100 marks, on paper at a registered school. It isn't required for Waterloo admission, but it feeds Faculty of Math scholarships.

Registration

Students can't register themselves. A school must order the contest through CEMC's Contest Supervisor Portal and supervise it.

Ask your own school's math department to register. If it won't, find a nearby school that already writes CEMC contests; you must write at the school that ordered it.

9–10Very hardFull solutionsProofs and extended problems; partial credit common

No calculus. Topics U.S. courses cover lightly: logarithms, trig identities and equations, Euclidean geometry, number theory, counting, and writing full solutions.

Study plan

For each paper: write it under contest conditions, mark it with the official solutions, rewrite every missed solution a day later, and keep a one-page formula and tricks sheet.

Test-day strategy

  • First 10 min: read all ten questions; mark what you can do
  • Q1–6 (about 75 min): steady work; double-check answer-only parts
  • Q7–10 (about 60 min): attempt every part; clear partial solutions earn marks
  • Last 15 min: check answers and solution write-ups
