University of Chicago

UChicago is test-optional with a "no harm" policy: scores are used only when they help. No interviews, but an optional two-minute video profile, due Jan 10, is highly recommended.

What to apply to

Apply to the College and list interests such as Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Physics or Molecular Engineering; the major is chosen later.
