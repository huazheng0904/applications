Personal Profile video practice

Runs the same format as the U of T Engineering Personal Profile: three random questions, 1 minute to prepare then 2 minutes to answer, 1 then 3, and 30 seconds then 3. Your webcam records each answer so you can watch it back. The practice questions are typical of this kind of assessment, not U of T's real ones. Works best in Chrome on a laptop.

Each answer is recorded on this computer, and in Chrome your words are transcribed automatically as you speak. Videos are never uploaded; Chrome's speech service processes the audio for the transcript. Recordings disappear when you close or reload the page, so save the ones you want: there is a Save link under each video and a Save all button.

Your answers

Watch each answer once. Check: does the first sentence answer the question, do you look at the camera, do you fill the time, and does the ending say what you learned?

All three videos save together as one .zip file in the Downloads folder; double-click it to unzip. Save before starting a new practice or closing the page: unsaved videos are lost.

Practice log

Every transcript is saved automatically in this browser on this laptop as he speaks, with the date and the questions. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing the browser's site data erases the log, so download it now and then.

Before the real one

Use the prep minute

Pick a story, jot three keywords: situation, what you did, what you learned. On Q4 you only get 30 seconds, so know your stories cold.

Problem-solving questions

There is no single right number. Say your assumptions out loud, break the problem into three steps, name the trade-offs, then give a rough answer and how you would check it.

Fill the time

Three minutes is long. End with what changed in you and how it connects to engineering, not a trailing "so yeah".

One attempt

The real assessment starts at "Start Assessment" and can't be paused or retaken. Practise here and on Kira's practice questions first. Use the same laptop, room and lighting you will use for the real one.
